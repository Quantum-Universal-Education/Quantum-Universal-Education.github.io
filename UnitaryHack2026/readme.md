# Game Rules

## Objective

Lead your flock of **Hens** to dominate the field before the **Quantum Hive Mind** and its flock of **Crows** do.

You win when **every bird remaining on the field is a Hen**.

The Quantum Hive wins when **every bird remaining on the field is a Crow**.

---

## Turn Structure

Each round consists of three phases:

### 1. Flap Phase

Both sides move one bird.

* The player selects a Hen and sends it across the fence.
* The Quantum Hive then selects and moves one Crow.
* The Hive's decision is determined by a quantum optimization circuit.

### 2. Breed Phase

Each side produces one chick.

* Two random birds from each side are selected as parents.
* The chick inherits feather genes from its parents.
* If both parents express the same gene, one parent's value is chosen randomly.
* If only one parent expresses a gene, that value is inherited.
* If neither parent expresses a gene, the gene remains unexpressed.

The chick's species is determined by the majority of its expressed genes:

* More Hen genes → Hen chick
* More Crow genes → Crow chick
* Tie → Randomly chosen

### 3. Molt Phase

One bird from each side retires from the flock.

* Older birds are more likely to be selected.
* This prevents flocks from growing forever and introduces natural turnover.

---

## Victory Conditions

### Hen Victory

The player wins when:

* Every bird on the field belongs to the Hen flock.

### Crow Victory

The Quantum Hive wins when:

* Every bird on the field belongs to the Crow flock.

### Ongoing Game

The game continues as long as both species still have at least one bird remaining.
