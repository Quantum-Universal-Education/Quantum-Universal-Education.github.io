Meet Qaly, the Quantum Cat.

A curious character helping make quantum computing easier to approach — one short animated explanation at a time.

Qaly is an educational project that brings together quantum concepts, visual storytelling, and a code-driven production workflow. The goal is not to simplify away the science, but to give people an inviting first step into it.

02 / How it is made

Creative output backed by code.

Each episode picks one concept and turns it into a picture a curious viewer can hold on to, without simplifying away the science. Scenes are animated in Python with Manim, and narration comes from Kokoro text-to-speech, so the series runs as a repeatable production pipeline rather than one-off clips.

  1. Concept what the viewer should understand
  2. Script a short narrative with accurate terms
  3. Animate Python + Manim
  4. Voice Kokoro TTS
  5. Publish and learn from audience questions
