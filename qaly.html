Meet Qaly the Quantum Cat.

Quantum ideas can feel abstract. Qaly turns them into curious questions, visual explanations, and short learning experiences—one concept at a time.

Explore Qaly on Instagram ↗How it is made

A personal educational experiment by Alberto Maldonado Romo.

Curiosity is a good starting state.

01 / The idea

Make the invisible easier to imagine.

Qaly is designed to make quantum computing feel less intimidating without hiding the underlying ideas. The character creates a friendly entry point; the explanations and visualizations do the teaching.

01

Curiosity first

Start with a question or surprising intuition, then introduce the concept.

02

Visual explanations

Use motion and diagrams to make states, gates, and quantum behavior easier to reason about.

03

Technical honesty

Keep the science accurate, distinguish analogy from formal explanation, and link learning back to code.

02 / Behind the scenes
