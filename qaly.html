Meet Qaly, the Quantum Cat.

A curious character helping make quantum computing easier to approach — one short animated explanation at a time.

Qaly is an educational project that brings together quantum concepts, visual storytelling, and a code-driven production workflow. The goal is not to simplify away the science, but to give people an inviting first step into it.

01 / The idea

Technical ideas, with a character people can follow.

Qaly gives the educational series a recognizable voice and visual identity, creating a bridge between abstract quantum concepts and approachable explanations.

What it teaches

Short explainers about quantum computing, hardware, and quantum gates. The format is designed for concise, visual learning rather than long-form lectures.

Who it is for

People who are curious about quantum computing and want a friendly entry point before moving into deeper technical material.

02 / Production workflow

A small educational media pipeline.

The project combines technical scripting with visual and audio production.

01 · RESEARCHChoose a concept

05 · PUBLISHShare and learn

Publish short explainers and improve the format over time.

03 / Technical foundation

Creative output backed by code.

Qaly currently brings together Python, Manim, and Kokoro text-to-speech in an open-source educational series of 60-second animated explainers. This makes the project more than a mascot: it is a repeatable content-production workflow that connects scientific communication with software tooling.
