Quantum Circuit Playground

Build a circuit on up to four qubits and watch the state vector, measurement probabilities, and Pauli expectation values update as you go. Export it as OpenQASM 2.0, see a qregime-style noise estimate, and share any circuit with a link.

Case study / Interactive product

Make circuit behavior inspectable while you build.

Quantum Circuit Playground is a browser-based environment for constructing small circuits and inspecting their simulated behavior without setting up a local quantum SDK.

01 · USER NEED

Reduce the setup barrier

Give learners and developers a quick way to experiment with gates, states, and measurements directly in the browser.

02 · INTERACTION

Connect edits to outcomes

The interface supports circuits of up to four qubits and updates the state vector, measurement probabilities, and Pauli expectation values as the circuit changes.

03 · WORKFLOW

Move beyond the interface

Export circuits as OpenQASM 2.0, share circuit configurations through links, and inspect a qregime-style noise estimate.

Scope note: the playground is a small-circuit educational simulator, not a replacement for hardware execution or a general-purpose quantum SDK. Its current feature set is described above; broader backend support is not implied.
