Quantum Circuit Playground

Build a circuit on up to four qubits and watch the state vector, measurement probabilities, and Pauli expectation values update as you go. Export it as OpenQASM 2.0, see a qregime-style noise estimate, and share any circuit with a link.

Product engineering case study

Make quantum circuits inspectable, testable, and shareable

User workflow

Build a circuit of up to four qubits, add gates, and inspect how the statevector, measurement probabilities, and Pauli expectation values change as the circuit evolves.

Technical surface

  • React-based browser interface
  • Statevector simulation and measurement probabilities
  • Pauli expectation values
  • OpenQASM 2.0 export
  • Noise estimate across hardware profiles
  • Shareable circuit links

Why it matters

The playground connects visual experimentation with a portable circuit representation. Users can inspect a circuit in the browser and export OpenQASM for a downstream workflow such as qregime.

Scope & limitations

The current interface is designed for up to four qubits. Its noise estimate is a lightweight exploratory aid, not a replacement for hardware calibration, a full noise simulation, or a performance guarantee.
